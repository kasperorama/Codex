Dear traveller,

Forget the water parks, the buffet queues and the sticky little fingers. Sjøgren's Travel takes grown-ups to Palma de Mallorca in golden September – when the sea is still warm, the grapes are coming in, and the city belongs to those of us who like a long lunch and a late night.

Three days. One departure. One small group of like-minded pleasure-seekers. We handle the flights, the hotel, the tables and the taxis – you handle the corkscrew.

Eat

From pa amb oli at the counter of Mercat de Santa Catalina to a long tasting menu under the stars. We'll feed you sobrassada, tumbet, frit mallorquí, grilled fish straight off the boat and, for breakfast, a sugar-dusted ensaïmada.

  • Guided tapas crawl through Santa Catalina & La Lonja
  • Market breakfast at Mercat de l'Olivar
  • Long lunch among the vines
  • Ensaïmada & cava farewell breakfast
🍷

Wine

September is verema – harvest time. We head inland to the D.O. Binissalem and Pla i Llevant to taste the island's own grapes: dark, spicy Manto Negro and Callet, and crisp white Prensal Blanc. Plus island vermouth, gin and cava on tap.

  • Bodega tour & tasting in Binissalem
  • Cava welcome on the roof terrace
  • Private driver – nobody has to drive home
  • Sunset vermouth hour
🪩

Pleasure

Sun loungers without cannonball splashes. A catamaran with an open bar. A rooftop pool, a beach club and a proper disco night. Our hotel is adults-only, and so is every one of our fellow travellers.

  • Sunset catamaran cruise, open bar
  • Beach club day with reserved day-beds
  • Rooftop pool at the hotel
  • Neon disco night in the old town

The Programme

Three days · 10–12 September 2027 · Subject to change, never to boredom

  1. FRI 10Morning flight Copenhagen → Palma. Check in at our adults-only boutique hotel in the old town. Welcome cava on the roof terrace, then a tapas crawl through Santa Catalina & La Lonja.
  2. SAT 11Market breakfast at Olivar. Harvest trip to Binissalem: bodega visits, tasting and a long lunch among the vines. Sunset catamaran cruise with open bar. Neon Disco Night.
  3. SUN 12Very late breakfast of ensaïmada & cava. Beach club day-beds or the rooftop pool, then the evening flight home, tanned and slightly heavier.

Meet the owner

Claus Sjøgren

a.k.a. “Mr Fromage”

Claus founded Sjøgren's Travel with one simple belief: a holiday should taste good. And nothing tastes better to Claus than cheese. He has such a deep love for it that friends, staff and guests only call him by one name: Mr Fromage.

Travel with Claus and you'll hear all about the tangy Mahón from neighbouring Menorca, the sheep's cheeses of the Mallorcan hills, and exactly which island wine to pour with each one. Don't be surprised if a cheese board appears when you least expect it.

“Wine without cheese is like Palma without sunshine.”
